About The Literary Society

The Literary Society is the heart of cultural and creative life at Methodist College of Engineering and Technology. As a club driving cultural activities, college fests, open mics, debates, and creative showcases, we provide a dynamic platform for everyone who loves reading, writing, public speaking, or performing.

Throughout the year, we organize and anchor major campus events, workshops, and interactive sessions designed to let your talents shine.

Why Join The Literary Society?

  • Drive Cultural Life: Help organize and participate in college fests, cultural celebrations, and flagship campus events.
  • Express Yourself: Showcase your writing, poetry, debating, anchoring, and public speaking skills.
  • Build Key Skills: Develop communication, leadership, stage presence, and event management abilities.
  • Connect & Collaborate: Join a welcoming community of thinkers, storytellers, and creators.
  • Take the Lead: Take charge of initiatives and society events.

Who Can Apply?

Every student at MCET, from any year or branch, is welcome to apply. All you need to bring is your passion, creativity, and enthusiasm!
