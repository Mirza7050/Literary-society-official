03 / KNOW THE RULES

TOURNAMENT RULEBOOK.

Read every rule carefully before registering.

01 DEVICE AND MERIT REQUIREMENTS
  • Merit below 80 = Direct kick/disqualification.
  • Low-level IDs must be informed to management beforehand. Management will not be responsible if such IDs are kicked.
  • Only mobile devices are allowed.
  • Emulators, iPads, tablets, and other non-mobile devices are prohibited.
02 RESULT SCREENSHOTS
  • Result screenshots are mandatory.
  • Management may request them at any time.
  • Failure to provide a screenshot means no points will be awarded.
03 ALLOTTED SLOTS
  • Players must sit in their allotted slots.
  • Management is not responsible if a player is kicked for failing to occupy the assigned slot.
04 ALL FOUR PLAYERS MUST JOIN
  • All four squad members must join before the last kick time.
  • If a team joins with fewer than four players and another team occupies its slot, management will not be responsible for any resulting disruption or free finishes.
05 ROOM AND MANAGEMENT ERRORS
  • If management fails to provide the IDP or mistakenly kicks a team and starts the match, the affected team will receive 4–5 compensation points. No bargaining will be accepted.
  • Due to network glitches, players may receive alerts such as "You have been removed from the room" or "Room has been disbanded".
  • Such alerts alone are not valid proof of being kicked.
  • Only a screenshot showing the waiting timer to rejoin the room, along with a clip showing entry of the IDP and password, will be accepted as valid proof of a management kick.
06 POV REQUIREMENTS
  • All players must record their complete RAW POV.
  • The recording must show:
  • Opening the game from the Play Store/App Store.
  • Apps running in the background.
  • The About Phone section from device settings.
07 POV AND DEATHCAM INVESTIGATION
  • When POV is requested, both the team's POV and deathcam must be provided mandatorily.
  • If management fails to provide the alleged team's POV, compensation may be awarded at management's discretion if the allegations are found genuine.
  • Compensation rules:
  • If the alleged team is not in the top 4, zero compensation points.
  • If the alleged team is in the top 4 and fails to submit POVs, 4 compensation points.
  • If further investigation confirms hacking, 1 point per player finished by that team will be awarded to teams that reported them.
  • Compensation points may vary depending on management's decision. No bargaining will be accepted.
08 SELF AED
  • Picking, attempting to use, or self-reviving using Self AED is prohibited.
  • Guilty teams receive zero points.
  • No compensation will be awarded to the victim.
09 EMERGENCY PICKUP AND MORTAR
  • Emergency pickup is not allowed.
  • Violation results in zero points for that game.
  • Mortar is not allowed.
  • Mortar usage results in zero points for the offending team and +5 points for the victim.
  • Deathcam is mandatory.
10 HACKING
  • Any player found guilty of hacking will receive a direct permanent ban.
11 DEVICE CHECKS AND INVESTIGATIONS
  • If a team faces allegations or anything appears suspicious, management reserves the right to request POVs or conduct a device check at any time.
  • A device check may be requested even after POV submission.
  • Failure to provide POV results in no PP.
  • In certain cases, zero points may be awarded instead of disqualification.
  • No excuses will be accepted.
12 TIE-BREAKER

In case of a tie, rankings will be decided in this order:

  1. Number of WWCDs.
  2. Position Points.
  3. Finishes Points.
  4. Last Match Position.
13 FINAL MANAGEMENT DECISION

In any dispute, unforeseen circumstance, or situation not covered by these rules, the management's decision will be final.

ADDITIONAL GUIDELINES

  1. No device-check requests will be accepted without POV.
  2. The About Phone section must be shown in the POV.
  3. No POV = Direct disqualification.
  4. No device check or argument will be entertained without POV.
